Aredo HR Privacy Policy

Responsible Organization: Brace Dev

Last Updated: November 1, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Aredo, a digital platform designed to manage and simplify human resources services and employee-related operations.

We are committed to respecting users' privacy and protecting their personal and employment-related information. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use, process, and store it, who may access it, and the rights users have regarding their information.

By using the Aredo application, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Aredo collects and processes information necessary to provide its human resources services, including:

Personal and Employment Information

  • Full name.
  • Employee ID or identification number.
  • Account and login information.
  • Contact information, such as phone number or email address, when provided.

Location and Attendance Information

  • Geographic location at the time of attendance and check-out registration.
  • Date and time of attendance and check-out records.
  • Attendance records associated with the employee's account.

Location information is used for attendance-related purposes and workplace verification, in accordance with application features and the permissions granted by the user.

Employee Request Information

  • Leave requests and related details.
  • Salary advance requests.
  • Request statuses and associated administrative approvals.

Financial and Employment Information

  • Employee rewards and bonuses.
  • Recorded deductions.
  • Salary advances and employment-related entitlements.

Technical and Device Information

  • Device type and operating system.
  • Device identifiers used for account security and device management.
  • Application version and technical diagnostic information.
  • Login records and error logs, when collected for application operation and security.

3. Face and Biometric Information

If face verification is enabled by the organization, Aredo may use the device camera to capture face images for identity verification and attendance security.

Face images may be securely transmitted to the application backend for verification. The verification process may include face detection, liveness checks, and facial feature matching.

Face and biometric information is used only for identity verification and related security purposes. It is not used for advertising.

Access to this information is restricted to authorized systems and personnel as necessary for the verification service.

4. How We Use Information

The collected information is used for the following purposes:

  • Creating and managing employee accounts.
  • Recording and monitoring attendance and check-out.
  • Verifying the geographic location associated with attendance records.
  • Submitting and managing leave requests.
  • Submitting and tracking salary advance requests.
  • Displaying and monitoring rewards and deductions.
  • Enabling authorized management personnel to oversee employee-related matters.
  • Performing face verification where enabled.
  • Improving application performance and maintaining system security.

5. Data Access and Sharing

Access to employee information is restricted to authorized management personnel (Admin), in accordance with the administrative permissions established within the organization.

Information may also be processed through cloud hosting and technical service providers required to operate the application and store its data.

Data is shared only to the extent necessary to provide application services and manage employment-related operations.

We do not sell users' personal information or rent it to third parties for marketing purposes.

6. Data Storage and Security

Application data is stored using cloud hosting services. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to help protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, loss, or misuse.

Access to employee information is controlled according to approved administrative permissions.

However, no method of data transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention

Personal and employment-related information is retained for as long as necessary to provide the application's services and fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, or for any period required by applicable legal, administrative, or regulatory obligations.

When information is no longer required, it will be deleted or anonymized in accordance with established procedures, unless there is a legal or administrative reason to retain it.

8. User Rights and Data Deletion Requests

Subject to applicable laws and regulations, users have the right to:

  • Inquire about their personal and employment-related information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Obtain information about how their data is used.
  • Request deletion of their personal information where permitted by applicable requirements.

How to Request Data Deletion: Users may submit a data deletion request by contacting the HR department of their respective organization or by emailing info@bracedev.com.

Requests will be reviewed and handled in accordance with applicable policies and legal and administrative obligations.

9. Application Permissions

Depending on the features enabled by the organization, Aredo may request:

  • Location (GPS): To register attendance and check-out and verify workplace location.
  • Camera: To capture face images when face verification is enabled.

These permissions are used for their stated application functions. Users can manage permissions through their device settings, although disabling them may prevent certain features from working.

10. Children's Privacy

Aredo is intended for employees and authorized administrative personnel. It is not directed toward children and does not target the collection of children's personal information.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update or modify this Privacy Policy when necessary due to changes in application services, data processing practices, or applicable legal requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page along with a revised update date.
