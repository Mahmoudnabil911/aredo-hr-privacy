Aredo HR Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 1, 2026

Aredo HR is an employee management application that helps organizations manage attendance, leave requests, financial requests, and other human resources services.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and processed when employees use Aredo HR.

1. Information We Collect

  • Employee information, including name, phone number, and employee ID.
  • Account credentials and device identifiers.
  • Location information for attendance and workplace verification.
  • Face images and biometric verification information when face verification is used.
  • Employment-related financial information, including advances, rewards, and deductions.
  • Photos or other information submitted by employees through the application.
  • Device and technical information required for application operation and security.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used to:

  • Authenticate employees and manage their accounts.
  • Record attendance and verify workplace location.
  • Process leave and financial requests.
  • Perform identity and face verification where enabled.
  • Maintain application security and prevent unauthorized access.
  • Provide HR services and support organizational operations.

3. Location Information

Location information may be used to verify that employees are within an organization's designated workplace area when using attendance and location-based verification features.

4. Face and Biometric Information

If face verification is enabled, the application may collect face images for identity verification and attendance security. Such information is processed for authentication purposes.

5. Data Sharing

Employee information may be accessible to authorized organization administrators for legitimate HR and employment purposes. Information may also be processed by service providers necessary to operate and secure the application, subject to applicable agreements and safeguards.

6. Data Security and Retention

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. Information is retained only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy and applicable requirements.

7. Your Rights

Employees may contact their organization's HR administrator to request access to, correction of, or information about their personal data, subject to applicable laws.

8. Children's Privacy

Aredo HR is designed for employees and organizational users and is not intended for children.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will be published on this page.

10. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions or requests, please contact your organization's HR administrator or the Aredo HR support team.
